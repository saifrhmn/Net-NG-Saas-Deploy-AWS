How to read this manualWords in blue boxes are exactly what you see on screen: menu items, buttons and field names. Icons such as edit are shown the same way they appear in Karbar. All amounts are in Taka (৳).

1Signing in

Opens automatically when you are not signed in.

  1. Enter your Phone Number Or Email and Password. Press visibility to show the password while you type.
  2. Press Login. The button shows Loading… while Karbar checks your details, then the Dashboard opens.

If the details are wrong you will see *** Phone Number or Password is incorrect under the password box.

Forgot your password

  1. Press Forgot Password? under the password box.
  2. Enter your Phone Number, a New Password, and the same password again in Confirm Password.
  3. Press Reset Password. The button stays disabled until both passwords match.
SessionsKarbar signs you out when your session expires. Sign in again and carry on; anything not yet saved on the open screen must be entered again.

2Finding your way

  • Left menu. Every screen opens from here. The menu button at the top left shrinks the menu to icons or opens it again. Hover over an icon to see its name. On a phone, the menu slides in from the left.
  • Reports. Click Reports to unfold the list of reports underneath it.
  • Your name appears at the top right. Click it and choose Logout to sign out. Logout is also at the bottom of the left menu.
  • Tabs. Sales, Purchase, Order and Settings split their work into tabs across the top, for example New Sales and Completed Sales.
  • Lists have a search box above and page controls below. Choose 10, 25, 50 or 100 rows per page.
  • Icons in lists. border_color or edit opens a record for changes, delete removes it, visibility shows it read-only.
  • Messages appear briefly in the corner of the screen after you save, or when something needs fixing.

4Dashboard

Menu: Dashboard. Opens after you sign in.

  • Today's figures: Today's Sale, Today's Customer Due, Today's Berger Purchase and Today's Non Berger Purchase.
  • List of Receivable: customers who owe you money, with amount and month.
  • Expense chart: press Today, 7 days or 30 days to compare spending. Point at a dot to see its exact amount.
  • Low Stock: items running low, with current stock in each warehouse. Reorder them through Order or Purchase.
  • User Manual: download this manual in English or Bangla at any time.

5Sales

Menu: Sales. Tabs: New Sales, Draft, Completed Sales, Sales Return.

New Sales→ Save → invoice preview→ Submit & Print or Draft→ Completed Sales→ Return, if needed

Make a sale

  1. On New Sales, check the Invoice No at the top. Leave it to be generated, type your own, or press sync to generate a new number. Change the Date if the sale was not made today.
  2. In Customer, type a name, phone number or address and pick the customer. For a new customer press add, fill in Customer Name, Contact No and Address, and save. Karbar warns Contact already exists! if that phone number is already registered.
  3. Choose the Warehouse the goods leave from.
  4. In Item Code, Name …, type part of the material number or name and click the item. It is added with its available stock and selling price. Type the Shades Name for tinted paint if needed.
  5. In the summary on the right, set each line's Quantity with remove / add or by typing. You can change the Price per line. Remove a line with delete.
  6. Fill in Discount (choose % or ৳), TAX (%), Delivery Charge and any Remarks. Karbar calculates Final Amount and Total Payable.
  7. Enter what the customer pays in Payment. Karbar shows the Due, or the Return (change to give back) if they paid more.
  8. Open the payment details and choose a Payment Method: Cash, Credit, Mobile Banking (pick bKash, Nagad, Rocket or Upay and enter the phone number) or Bank Transfer (pick the bank and enter the account number).
  9. Press Save. The sales invoice preview opens. Check it, then press Submit & Print to complete the sale, or Draft to finish it later.
Not enough stockIf you see One or more items do not have sufficient stock in the selected warehouse, lower the quantity, choose another warehouse, or move stock first with Shift Stock (section 12).

The printed invoice shows your shop logo, address and signature from your user profile, the amount in words, and the line “Color Bank products are non-returnable.”

Work with drafts

The Draft tab lists saved, unfinished sales. For each draft:

  • edit Edit draft loads it back into New Sales to change it.
  • send Send to Sales completes it as a sale.
  • delete Delete draft removes it.

Find a completed sale

On Completed Sales, set the From and To dates to narrow the list, or press Clear to show everything. Press visibility on a row to open its invoice, then Print to reprint it. Reprinted invoices list returned items and deduct them from the total.

6Sales returns

Menu: Sales → Completed Sales tab.

  1. Find the invoice and tick the item lines the customer is bringing back.
  2. In Change Quantity, enter how many units come back. It cannot be more than the quantity sold.
  3. Press undo in the Return column, write the reason in Remarks, and press Submit.

Returned lines show Returned with check_circle, and the stock goes back to the warehouse. All returns are listed on the Sales Return tab and in the Sales Return report.

7Purchase

Menu: Purchase. Tabs: New Purchase, Completed Purchase. Use this for stock bought without a Berger SAP invoice.

  1. Make sure the switch at the top (tooltip Berger SAP invoice) is off. Turned on, it takes you to Berger SAP invoices (section 8).
  2. Enter the supplier's Invoice No. It is required.
  3. Choose the Supplier. For a new supplier press add and fill in Supplier Name, Contact, Address and Status.
  4. Choose the Warehouse receiving the goods, then search for and click each item.
  5. In the summary, set Quantity, MRP and PR. Price (the price you paid) for each line.
  6. Fill in Discount (% or ৳), TAX (%) and VAT (%), the amount paid in Payment, and the Payment Method.
  7. Press Save, check the purchase summary, and press Submit. The stock is added to the chosen warehouse.

Saved purchases appear on Completed Purchase with quantity, price, discount, tax and VAT for each line. Pay any remaining supplier due from Payment (section 10).

8Berger SAP invoices

Menu: Purchase, then turn on the switch at the top. The title changes to Purchase Details. Karbar remembers the switch position next time.

Use this when goods arrive from Berger against an SAP invoice. The invoice lines are already in Karbar; you confirm what actually arrived.

  1. Type the invoice number in Berger Invoice No and pick it from the suggestions.
  2. On Purchase List, choose the Warehouse for every line. The invoice cannot be received until every line has a warehouse.
  3. Enter the Receive Quantity for each line, up to the invoiced quantity, and check the Unit Price.
  4. Press arrow_forward on the row to receive it. You will see Received quantity updated.

If only part of an invoice arrived, the rest waits on Pending Purchase with its Remaining Quantity. Receive it there with arrow_forward when it arrives. Fully received invoices move to Completed Purchase.

9Order from Berger

Menu: Order. Tabs: New Order, Order List.

Use this to request stock from Berger. The supplier is fixed to Berger.

  1. On New Order, choose the Warehouse the goods should go to. It is required.
  2. Search for items and click to add them. The table shows each item's current stock to help you decide how much to order.
  3. Set the Quantity per line. The purchase price is fixed and filled in for you.
  4. Press Save, check the order summary, and press Submit.

Order List shows each order's items and status: Pending until it is received, then Received. Administrators see the dealer name on each order and change the status from the drop-down. A Received order can no longer be changed.

10Payments and dues

Menu: Payment.

Collect money customers owe you, or pay what you owe suppliers.

  1. In Select Type, choose Customer (money coming in) or Supplier (money going out).
  2. Search by Invoice No, Name, Phone and pick the result. The list shows each invoice's total, paid and due amounts.
  3. On an invoice with a due amount, press arrow_forward (View Details).
  4. In Payment Details, choose the payment method, enter any additional discount, and enter the amount being paid. It cannot be more than the due amount.
  5. Press Submit, then Print Receipt if a receipt is needed.

Fully paid invoices show print instead, for reprinting.

11Expenses

Menu: Expense Information.

  1. Press Create Expense.
  2. Set the Expense Date. The entry date is filled in as today.
  3. Choose the Expense Category. To add one, press add, enter a Category Name, and tick Have parent? if it belongs under another category (for example “Electricity” under “Utilities”).
  4. Enter the Expense Amount and Expense Purpose, then save.

Use border_color or delete to correct or remove an entry. Expenses also appear in the Dashboard expense chart.

12Stock Management

Menu: Stock Management.

One row per item, one column per warehouse, and a Total Quantity column.

  • Find items with the search box. Use Sort / Filter for A→Z, Z→A, Low Stock, High Stock or Safety Stock, and tick Not Include 0 stock to hide empty items.
  • History: each item shows its Last Purchase (supplier, quantity, amount) and Last Sell (customer, quantity, amount).

Correct a stock count

  1. Double-click the quantity in the warehouse column and type the counted figure.
  2. Press Save on that row, enter Remarks (for example “Physical count 12 Jan”), and press Submit.

Move stock between warehouses

  1. Press compare_arrows beside the search box to open Shift Stock. (Not available to shop staff.)
  2. Choose the Item Name. Karbar shows how much is in each warehouse.
  3. Choose From Ware House and To Ware House, enter the Quantity and Remarks, and press Submit.

Every transfer appears in the Stock Transfer report.

13Items

Menu: Item.

Your product list. Switch between cards and a table with view_list / grid_view. Search by item code, and narrow by Choose Brand and Choose Category.

Add an item

  1. Press add beside the search box.
  2. Enter Item Name, Item Description and Material Number. Karbar suggests existing names and numbers as you type, to help avoid duplicates.
  3. Choose the brand, category and Unit. If one is missing, press add beside it to create it.
  4. Enter Safety Stock (the minimum level you want to keep), MRP, Purchase Price and Selling Price. Weighted Average Price is calculated by Karbar.
  5. Optionally upload a product image, then save.

Upload many items at once (administrators)

  1. Press cloud_download to download the Excel template.
  2. Fill in one item per row. Rows without an item name or material number are skipped.
  3. Press upload and pick the file (.xlsx, .xls or .csv). Karbar reports how many items it uploaded.

View prices

Press visibility on an item to see its details. Prices are hidden: press View Price Details and enter your password to show MRP, selling, purchase and weighted average prices.

15Users

Menu: Users. Administrators only.

  1. Press Create User.
  2. Enter User Name, Address Line 1, Address Line 2 and Phone Number. The user signs in with this phone number.
  3. Choose the Role. For dealers, enter their SAP Code.
  4. Upload a Logo and Signature. These print on the user's sales invoices.
  5. Set a Password and Status, then save.

To give someone a new password, press lock_outline on their row, enter it twice and press Reset Password.
